Public Finance · Health Policy · State & Local Government

Lady Ikeya

Ph.D. Candidate in Public Affairs, Paul H. O’Neill School of Public and Environmental Affairs, Indiana University Bloomington.

I study how intergovernmental fiscal rules shape what state and local governments actually spend — from Medicaid provider taxes and federal deduction caps to the way short‑term rental markets reshape municipal revenue. I also teach undergraduate public budgeting and finance at the O’Neill School.

Teaching

In the classroom

SPEA V186 · LEC 7431 Spring 2026

Introduction to Public Budgeting and Finance

A first course in government finance: the budget cycle and its politics, revenue systems and tax incidence, debt and capital budgeting, and the intergovernmental transfers that tie federal, state, and local budgets together. Students work directly with published budget and financial-report data.
